Macro

Your phone swallowed the camera, the music player, the map and the address book. Macro is doing that to work software.

Email, chat, docs, tasks, calls, a customer list and AI agents in one fast window, where anything can be @-linked to anything else. The team has run its own company on it for two years.

Why it's credible

  • Andreessen Horowitz led the $9.3M seed in 2023 and the $12M Series A in 2024. BoxGroup, Craft Ventures and 3kVC are also in.
  • Fully open source under AGPL since August. Not open core. The whole product, every service, readable today.
  • One of the top trending repos on GitHub the month it launched.
  • SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, at fifteen people.

Why now

Most companies pay for a dozen tools that don't talk to each other, and AI agents are only useful when they can see all of it. Macro bet that one shared system beats eleven integrations, and shipped the whole thing in the open.

The founders

Jacob Beckerman, Founder and CEO. Started Macro in New York in 2020, straight out of college. The first product was an AI document tool for law and finance. He rebuilt it into a full workspace and open-sourced it.

Teo Nys, Co-founder and CTO. Joined in 2023 as a senior engineer, ran engineering, and was made co-founder in 2025. Still writes code. His own GitHub is small Rust tools he built for himself. He reads yours the same way.

The role

A generalist seat, three of them. You take a feature end to end across the Rust services and the SolidJS client, anywhere in the product: mail, docs, sync, tasks, calls. Everyone's title is Software Engineer. What you ship lands on GitHub, in public, with your name on the commits.

What Teo is looking for

The one he won't bend on
Something you built for yourself that he can open.

Home-cooked software. The small tool that fixed your own daily annoyance, the editor plugin, the dotfiles, the toy you made because you wanted to. Many small things beat one showpiece. A plain personal site that loads counts.

  • Two to twelve years shipping at a real job, front and back. Small companies nobody has heard of are a plus, not a minus.
  • Rust by choice is the strongest plus. SolidJS, Tauri, Lexical, CRDTs or local-first work rank you up. None of it is required.
  • A generalist. Infrastructure only or front end only is not this seat.
  • Probably not a fit: a career mostly in crypto, a resume with nothing outside the day job, or a need for hybrid.
