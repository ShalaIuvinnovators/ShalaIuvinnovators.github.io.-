Company profile

GC AI builds legal AI for in-house legal teams, accurate enough for a lawyer to sign their name to it. Founded 2023 by Cecilia Ziniti, a three-time general counsel and the founding lawyer on Amazon Alexa, and CTO Bardia Pourvakil, building on language models since GPT-2. Revenue went from $1 million to more than $10 million in under a year, a $60 million Series B at a $555 million valuation behind it.

Product

The product reads a legal document, answers questions, redlines it and drafts from a template, inside the tools a legal team already uses. The lawyer using it cannot debug the answer, so it has to be right or say plainly that it is not sure. It runs four to five models tuned for different tasks, releasing weekly, so evaluating and shipping each one safely is a full team's job.

Tech stack

TypeScript, JavaScript, Next.js, Node.js, React, PostgreSQL, Google Cloud. LLM orchestration, retrieval, prompt and model choice, and evaluation across four to five models.

Ownership

Day one, an AI feature end to end: the orchestration, the retrieval, the prompt and model choice, the evaluation that proves it works, and the React surface a lawyer touches. On every model release, the evaluation and testing pass across the four or five models the product runs, and the call on whether a new one goes in. By month six, the repeatable frameworks for prototyping against a weekly model cadence, plus reviewing the pull requests the in-house lawyers submit.

Why them
