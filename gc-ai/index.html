Company profile

GC AI builds legal AI for in-house legal teams, accurate enough for a lawyer to sign their own name to it. Founded in 2023 by Cecilia Ziniti, a three-time general counsel and the founding lawyer on Amazon Alexa, and CTO Bardia Pourvakil, who has been building on language models since GPT-2. Revenue went from $1 million to more than $10 million in under a year. Investors took notice and decided it was a high-worth bet. This small but elite team raised a $60 million Series B, backed by a $555 million valuation.

Product

The product reads a legal document, answers questions, redlines it, and drafts from a template, inside the tools a legal team already uses. The lawyer using it cannot debug the answer, so it must be right or clearly translate the constraints. It runs four to five models tuned for different tasks and releases weekly, so evaluating and safely shipping each one is a full team's job.

Tech stack

TypeScript, JavaScript, Next.js, Node.js, React, PostgreSQL, Google Cloud. LLM orchestration, retrieval, prompt and model choice, and evaluation across four to five models.

Ownership

Day one, an AI feature end to end: the orchestration, the retrieval, the prompt and model choice, the evaluation that proves it works, and the React surface a lawyer touches. On every model release, the evaluation and testing pass across the four or five models the product runs, and the call on whether a new one goes in. By month six, the repeatable frameworks for prototyping against a weekly model cadence, plus reviewing the pull requests the in-house lawyers submit.

Why them
