Company snapshot

The Better Money Company is building a stablecoin clearinghouse. Banks, fintechs and payments companies swap one stablecoin for another one-for-one, with a fixed fee and a guaranteed settlement time, from a compliant source of funds. Today that swap means a trading venue with slippage or a manual chain of conversions, and the company is fixing it for businesses that move real money.

Why the market is watching: a16z crypto led the $10M seed. Ramp and Modern Treasury are design partners, BitGo is the custodian, a Circle co-founder is an angel, and the team was moving real money for customers within six months. Co-founder ran legal at Eigen Labs, so the licensing and regulation side is owned by the founders. The company is the neutral layer between issuers, which is what the larger players cannot be.

The founder

Sam Broner was a distributed systems engineer at Microsoft, worked at the Boston Fed, then spent three years at a16z looking at hundreds of stablecoin companies before deciding to build one. He is an engineer who became an investor and then went back to building.
