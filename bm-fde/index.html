The company

The Better Money Company is building the clearing layer for stablecoins. In the 1800s every bank issued its own dollar, and clearinghouses made them interchangeable. Stablecoins have the same problem today: every issuer's dollar is a different token. Better Money lets banks, fintechs and payment companies send any stablecoin in and get any stablecoin out, one-for-one, with a fixed fee and a guaranteed settlement time.

Why it's credible

  • $10M seed led by a16z crypto, with BoxGroup and Sunflower Capital
  • Angels include Circle co-founder Sean Neville and Patrick McHenry, former chair of the House Financial Services Committee
  • Issuers on board include Paxos, Stripe's Bridge, MoonPay, MetaMask and Phantom
  • Ramp and Modern Treasury are design partners
  • Custody is with BitGo, a publicly traded company that runs a federally regulated trust bank

Why now

The GENIUS Act made stablecoins regulated U.S. money, and companies like Klarna, Cloudflare, Sony and Fiserv are launching their own. Every new coin adds to the fragmentation problem this company solves.
