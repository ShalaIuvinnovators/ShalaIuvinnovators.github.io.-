Hello, a short brief made for you

This is not a job description. I'm writing to a small group of engineers who run the systems where the numbers can't be wrong, and I'd like you to be able to judge in two minutes whether a call is worth your time.

Company profile

The Better Money Company is a stablecoin clearinghouse. Banks, fintechs, and payments companies swap one stablecoin for another, one-for-one, across chains and issuers, with guaranteed settlement times and fixed fees. It supports 8 of the top 10 issuance platforms, and Ramp and Modern Treasury are design partners.

Product

Crypto today is built around trading, and those guarantees don't work for banks and payments companies. It's as if you had to sell Chase dollars to buy Bank of America dollars. The clearinghouse is the piece that makes stablecoins move the way money is supposed to move.

The bet

Sam Broner spent three years at a16z investing in stablecoins, after the Boston Fed and time as an engineer at Microsoft. He stopped funding the idea and left to build it, and within six months of starting the company was moving money for customers. People want what they're selling: new customers coming through the door now are payment companies and financial institutions, and the plan is to bring in more enterprise over time.

Tech stack

The muscle: settlement, reconciliation, ledgers and audit trails, pre-funding, and moving money through aging bank systems without dropping a cent, on a distributed state machine that has to stay right as it grows.

Ownership

The team is tight on purpose, with a lot of autonomy. Two or three engineering hires are planned for this month, and those who join early will receive more equity. Within six weeks, the goal is to own something the team hasn't had time to build.

Why them

You already own the part of a fund where the numbers can't be wrong. Here, that part isn't the back office. It's the company.
