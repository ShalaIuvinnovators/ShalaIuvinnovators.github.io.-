Hello, a short brief

I'm writing to a small group of engineers who look like they map well to an elite startup that's doing something innovative in the payments space, and I'd like you to be able to judge in two minutes whether a call is worth your time.

Ownership

The fiat and stablecoin clearing system, end to end: the ledger, the reconciliation and audit trail that keep every balance provably right, and the bank, issuer, and custodian integrations behind them. The team is tight on purpose, with a lot of autonomy. Two or three engineering hires are planned for this month, and those who join early will receive more equity.

At a large payments company you own a rail or a slice of the ledger and never see the bank contract, the reconciliation break, and the customer at once. Here you see every part. This role looks like the next logical step for you.

Company

The Better Money Company is a stablecoin clearinghouse: banks, fintechs and payments companies swap one stablecoin for another, one-for-one, with guaranteed settlement times and fixed fees.

Crypto today is built around trading, and today's rails don't work for banks moving real money. The clearinghouse is the missing piece: a licensed money transmitter with a regulated custodian and a compliance officer already in the building.

Sam Broner spent three years investing in stablecoins at a16z, then left to build it himself; the company was moving money for customers within six months, and generated $1.5M in its first week live.

Tech stack

The ledger and its reconciliation, pre-funding and settlement, and the connections into banks, stablecoin issuers and custodians, often over aging and fragmented APIs, without dropping a cent. TypeScript, Python, Node, PostgreSQL on the cloud. Stablecoin knowledge is teachable; this is not what wins the seat.

Salary

Up to $225K base. Early engineers leverage a negotiable, high-equity upswing.
