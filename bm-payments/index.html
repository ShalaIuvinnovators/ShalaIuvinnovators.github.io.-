Hello, a short brief made for you

This is not a job description. I'm writing to a small group of engineers who own the systems that move money and keep the books right, and I'd like you to be able to judge in two minutes whether a call is worth your time.

Company profile

The Better Money Company is a stablecoin clearinghouse. Banks, fintechs, and payments companies swap one stablecoin for another, one-for-one, across chains and issuers, with guaranteed settlement times and fixed fees. It supports 8 of the top 10 issuance platforms, and Ramp and Modern Treasury are design partners.

Product

Crypto today is built around trading, and those guarantees don't work for banks and payments companies. It's as if you had to sell Chase dollars to buy Bank of America dollars. The clearinghouse is the piece that makes stablecoins move the way money is supposed to move: a licensed money transmitter, a regulated custodian behind it, and a compliance officer already in the building.

The bet

Sam Broner spent three years at a16z investing in stablecoins, after the Boston Fed and time as an engineer at Microsoft. He stopped funding the idea and left to build it, and within six months of starting the company was moving money for customers. People want what they're selling: new customers coming through the door now are payment companies and financial institutions, and the plan is to bring in more enterprise over time.

Tech stack

The muscle: the ledger and its reconciliation, pre-funding and settlement, and the connections into banks, stablecoin issuers, and custodians, often across aging and fragmented APIs, without dropping a cent. TypeScript and Python, Node, and PostgreSQL on the cloud. Stablecoin knowledge is teachable and is not the thing that wins the seat.

Ownership

The fiat and stablecoin clearing system, end to end: the ledger, the reconciliation and audit trail that keep every balance provably right, and the bank, issuer, and custodian integrations behind them. The team is tight on purpose, with a lot of autonomy. Two or three engineering hires are planned for this month, and those who join early will receive more equity. Within six weeks, the goal is to own something whole that the team hasn't had time to build.

Why them

At a large payments company you own a rail or a slice of the ledger and never see the bank contract, the reconciliation break, and the customer at once. Here you see every part, and you own it.

Salary

Up to $220K base. Early engineers leverage a negotiable, high-equity upswing.
